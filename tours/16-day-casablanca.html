16 Days Morocco Tour from Casablanca

16 Days / 15 Nights 4.9 (112 reviews) Route: Casablanca → Rabat → Tangier → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga → Dades Gorges → Marrakech → Essaouira

Day-by-Day Program Itinerary

Day 1

Casablanca Arrival & Atlantic View

Private airport pickup and transfer to your luxury beachfront hotel. Relax and unwind as you acclimate to the beautiful Mediterranean climate, enjoying a fresh seafood dinner at the port harbor.

Day 2

Casablanca Hassan II Mosque to Rabat Capital

Tour the breathtaking Hassan II Mosque sitting directly over the Atlantic Ocean waves. In the afternoon, take a scenic drive to the capital city of Rabat to explore the historic Hassan Tower and Mausoleum.

Day 3

Rabat to Coastal Tangier via Asilah

Drive north along the scenic Atlantic coastline, stopping in the charming whitewashed art-filled town of Asilah. Continue past coastal cliffs to the legendary international gateway of Tangier, where the Mediterranean meets the Atlantic.

Day 4

Tangier City Tour & Cap Spartel

Explore the legendary Caves of Hercules and Cap Spartel lighthouse. Wander the labyrinth pathways of Tangier's old Kasbah, overlooking the Gibraltar Strait, and visit the historic American Legation museum.

Day 5

Tangier to Blue-Washed Chefchaouen

Drive past the dramatic limestone peaks of the Rif Mountains to the dreamy blue-painted town of Chefchaouen. Check into your zellige-trimmed luxury Riad and enjoy a relaxing sunset walk.

Day 6

Chefchaouen Medina Walking Tour

Wander the dreamy blue alleys of the medina, discovering local weaving cooperatives, hidden Andalusian gardens, and climbing up to the Spanish Mosque for a panoramic sunset view over the valley.

Day 7

Chefchaouen to Fes via Roman Ruins

Depart the Rif mountains, stopping to tour the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis and the historic gates of imperial Meknes. Check into a majestic medieval palace Riad inside the Fes medina.

Day 8

Fes Guided Medina History Tour

Explore Fes El Bali medina, a UNESCO site with over 9,000 alleys. Visit the 9th-century Al-Qarawiyyin University, Bou Inania Madrasa zellige work, and the royal palace gates.

Day 9

Fes Historical Palaces & Cooking Class

Discover historic Jewish heritage at the Mellah. Settle into a traditional hands-on Moroccan cooking class inside a boutique Riad, learning to roll couscous and prepare slow-cooked lamb tagines.

Day 10

Fes to Midelt Cedar Forests

Cross the Middle Atlas ranges, touring the alpine town of Ifrane and meeting native Barbary apes in the cedar forests of Azrou. Settle in for a peaceful evening in the quiet mountain town of Midelt.

Day 11

Midelt to Sahara Desert Dunes

Drive through the spectacular palm oases of the Ziz Valley, reaching the golden sands of Merzouga. Mount your camel for a scenic sunset trek into the heart of the Erg Chebbi luxury camp.

Day 12

Sahara Off-Road Nomad Safari

Spend a full day exploring the desert. Visit nomadic families in woolen tents, listen to spiritual Gnawa musicians in Khamlia, eat delicious Berber pizza, and sleep under bright desert stars.

Day 13

Merzouga to Dades Gorges via Todra

Witness sunrise over the dunes. Walk beneath vertical cliffs at Todra Gorge and wind past strange geological rock formations to your panoramic luxury stone Riad in the Dades Gorges.

Day 14

Dades Valley to Red Marrakech

Explore Ait Benhaddou mud-brick Kasbah, then cross the High Atlas peaks via Tizi n'Tichka pass. Arrive in Marrakech and enjoy the energetic street performers at Jemaa el-Fnaa square.

Day 15

Marrakech Medina Secrets Guided Tour

Tour the beautiful Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs, and towering Koutoubia Mosque. Stroll Majorelle's cobalt blue pathways and shop for leather, brass, and spices inside colorful craft souks.

Day 16

Essaouira Atlantic Port to Casablanca Departure

Depart to Essaouira coastal town to tour old Portuguese sea fortress walls. Settle in for fresh harbor seafood before transferring back to Casablanca airport for your return flight home.
